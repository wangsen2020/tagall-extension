# Product positioning

## Problem

WhatsApp's native `@all` is the fastest option when it is available and a user has permission to use it. In practice, people who operate or promote in a group may not have that permission, may be in a rollout cohort without the command, or may need to mention a selected set of people rather than the whole group.

## Product promise

**TagAll prepares individual mentions when native `@all` is unavailable to you.**

The product must not claim to unlock WhatsApp's native feature or evade an administrator's permissions. It saves the manual work of selecting members one by one and leaves the message unsent for human review.

## Primary audience

- Community operators and organizers who are not group administrators
- Promoters who send relevant, opt-in announcements to groups they participate in
- Support or sales teams coordinating time-sensitive, group-specific updates

## MVP guardrails

- No automatic message sending
- A visible red stop icon during mention preparation; it stops before the next member is added
- A moderate default insertion delay
- Local-only processing of participant labels
- Clear reminders to use mentions only for relevant, consented communication

## Future, user-controlled value

- Mention only selected participants rather than everyone
- Draft templates with variables such as event date or discount code
- Per-group template shortcuts stored locally
- Pre-send checklist for links, language, and intended audience
